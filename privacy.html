CloudSSD Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

CloudSSD is a Windows desktop application that allows users to access their Google Drive files through a virtual drive interface in Windows File Explorer.

This Privacy Policy explains how CloudSSD accesses and handles information when users connect their Google accounts.

2. Information and Google Drive Access

CloudSSD uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate users. Users choose whether to grant the application access to their Google Drive account.

The application currently requests read-only Google Drive access. It uses the Google Drive API to retrieve file and folder information and access file content where required by its functionality.

CloudSSD does not request permission to create, modify, rename or delete files in Google Drive.

3. Authentication and Local Storage

OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's Windows computer so that the application can maintain its Google account connection.

CloudSSD also uses local storage for file caching and application-related data.

4. Data Sharing

CloudSSD does not operate a separate cloud service for storing users' Google Drive files. Google Drive requests are made through Google's APIs.

Information is transmitted to Google as necessary to authenticate users and provide the application's Google Drive functionality.

5. Data Security

Users should protect their Windows accounts and devices because locally stored authentication tokens and cached information may be accessible to anyone with sufficient access to those devices.

6. Revoking Access

Users can revoke CloudSSD's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings:

Manage Google Account permissions

7. Children's Privacy

CloudSSD is not specifically designed for children. Users must comply with Google's applicable account and age requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CloudSSD's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
